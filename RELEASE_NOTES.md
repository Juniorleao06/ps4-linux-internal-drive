# v1.0.0 — Initial experimental release candidate

- Rebranded boot/recovery initramfs as public v1.
- Documented internal-HDD CachyOS boot, existing-image detection, and guarded password recovery.
- Included the exact tested Baikal `bzImage` for local archival and provenance review.
- Documented observed AMD Liverpool OpenGL 4.5 and RADV Vulkan 1.3, including successful `vkcube` rendering.
- Added installation, recovery, compatibility, credits, and safety documentation.

**Release status:** Experimental, unstable, tested on one PS4 Slim Baikal only.

**Publication gate:** Verify redistribution rights, firmware licenses, kernel corresponding source, and the provenance of the PS4-compatible CachyOS root filesystem before publicly releasing binaries.
