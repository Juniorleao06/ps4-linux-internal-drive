Contributing

Thank you for your interest in PS4 Linux Internal Drive — Baikal Edition.

This is an experimental community project documenting Linux running from an ext4 loopback image on the PlayStation 4 internal HDD.

How to Contribute

Contributions are welcome in the following areas:

- Testing on additional PS4 hardware revisions
- Improving installation and recovery documentation
- Reporting reproducible bugs
- Identifying upstream sources and licenses
- Reviewing scripts for safety
- Improving compatibility information

Reporting Issues

Please include:

- PS4 model and hardware revision
- PS4 firmware version
- Linux kernel version
- Linux distribution
- Steps to reproduce the problem
- Relevant error messages

Do not include passwords, private keys or personal information.

Safety Requirements

- Never recommend formatting the entire PS4 internal HDD.
- Clearly identify commands that may erase data.
- Do not present untested procedures as verified.
- Do not submit third-party binaries without checking their provenance and redistribution licenses.
